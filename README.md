# Ferns-and-Petals-Gifting-brand-Dashboard

An interactive Power BI sales analysis dashboard built to understand
sales performance, customer ordering patterns, product performance,
occasion-wise revenue, and geographic demand for Ferns & Petals
(FNP).

📊 Project Overview

This project converts raw sales/order data into an interactive business
intelligence dashboard. The dashboard provides a high-level view of
revenue and order performance while allowing users to filter the
analysis by:

Category

Occasion

Location

The goal is to identify revenue trends, high-performing products,
important occasions, and cities with strong order volumes.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Power BI -- Dashboard development and visualization

Power Query -- Data cleaning and transformation

DAX -- Measures and KPI calculations

Excel/CSV Dataset -- Source data

📈 Dashboard Analysis

1. Revenue by Month

The monthly revenue trend helps identify seasonal fluctuations and
high-revenue periods.

The dashboard shows noticeable peaks during February and August,
while revenue is comparatively lower across several other months.

2. Top Products by Revenue

The product-level analysis highlights the products generating the
highest revenue.

The dashboard displays the Top 10 products by revenue, making it
easier to identify products that contribute significantly to overall
sales.

3. Revenue Across Occasions

Revenue is compared across different occasions, including:

Anniversary

Raksha Bandhan

All Occasions

Holi

Birthday

Valentine's Day

Diwali

This view helps understand how different gifting occasions contribute to
overall business revenue.

4. Top 10 Cities by Orders

💡 Business Insights

Based on the dashboard:

The business generated approximately ₹35.2 lakh in revenue
across 1,000 orders.

The average order value is approximately ₹3,521, indicating the
average customer transaction size.

Total quantity sold is 3,045 units.

Revenue varies considerably across months, indicating seasonal
demand.

Certain gifting occasions contribute substantially to revenue and
can be useful for seasonal marketing campaigns.

A relatively small group of products generates a significant share
of revenue, making product-level analysis useful for inventory and
promotional decisions.

City-level order analysis can help identify locations with stronger
demand and potential opportunities for targeted marketing.
